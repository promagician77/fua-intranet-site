Find what SharePoint will refuse, before anything moves

Your existing forms, guidelines and marketing files are probably in folders on a shared drive or in OneDrive. SharePoint refuses some names and paths outright. This check lists them and writes a rename plan you can review.

Check a folder on this computer

A folder you choose is read in this browser tab for names and sizes only. No file is opened or uploaded.

The agent question

Many intranets are shared with "Everyone except external users". That built-in group covers every account in your Microsoft 365 and no guest account. If any agent signs in with an outside email, they are a guest.
